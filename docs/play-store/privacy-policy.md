## 1. About the app

Asset Recovery is a business application for authorised field agents of
Sun Mobility Private Limited and its partners. Agents use it to locate, verify and recover
company-owned assets (such as batteries) from customers, and to record the
outcome of each recovery attempt. It is not intended for the general public or
for anyone under 18.

## 2. Information we collect

| Information | Why we collect it |
|---|---|
| Agent mobile number and one-time password (OTP) | To sign you in and verify you are an authorised agent. |
| Agent profile (name, agent ID, role, assigned area) | To show your assigned tickets and attribute your actions. |
| Precise location (GPS) | To confirm you are at the customer or asset location (geo-fence check), to attach location to proof, and for navigation. Collected only while the app is open (foreground). Not collected in the background. |
| Photos taken in the app | To record proof of a recovery attempt. Photos may carry the location where they were taken. |
| Scanned QR / serial codes | To identify the asset being recovered. |
| Customer and asset details shown on tickets (name, phone number, address, asset serial, dues) | Supplied by Sun Mobility Private Limited; displayed so you can contact and visit the customer. |
| Call information (customer number dialled, call start time and duration) | To log that a customer call took place and for how long. The app does not record call audio. |
| Attempt records and audit log (ticket ID, outcome, notes, timestamps, screens used) | To keep an accountable history of each recovery attempt. |
| Device / app data held on the phone (session token, cached tickets, queued uploads, language) | To keep you signed in and let you work with poor connectivity. Stored on the device; the sign-in session is kept in encrypted storage. |

We do not use advertising SDKs and do not sell personal information.

## 3. Permissions the app asks for

- **Location** — geo-fence verification, proof and navigation.
- **Camera** — proof photos and QR scanning.
- **Phone (call) and phone state** — to place the customer call from your SIM and
  measure its real duration. Without the phone-state permission calls still work and
  the app times them itself.
- **Internet / network state** — to talk to our servers and detect offline mode.

You can withdraw any permission in Android settings; some features will stop working.

## 4. Who we share information with

We share data only as needed to run the service, with:

- **Microsoft Azure** — hosts our APIs and stores uploaded proof photos.
- **Gupshup** — delivers the sign-in OTP SMS to your mobile number.
- **Google (Google Maps)** — displays maps and navigation.
- **Sun Mobility Private Limited and its authorised partners** — the organisation that employs or
  contracts you, for operational and compliance purposes.
- **Authorities**, where required by law.

Data is sent over HTTPS.
