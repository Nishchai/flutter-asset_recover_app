# Play Store listing (draft)

**App name (30 max):** Asset Recovery
**Short description (80 max):**
Field-agent app to locate, verify and recover company assets with proof.

**Full description (4000 max):**
Asset Recovery is the field companion for authorised Sun Mobility Private Limited recovery agents.

- Work your assigned recovery tickets, with live and cached status
- Navigate to the customer and verify you are on site with GPS geo-fencing
- Call the customer from the app and have the call duration logged
- Scan the asset QR / serial to confirm the right unit
- Capture geo-tagged proof photos and record each attempt outcome
- Keeps working with weak signal: attempts and photos queue offline and sync later
- Available in multiple languages

This app is for authorised agents only. Sign in with your registered mobile
number and OTP. Contact your supervisor if you do not have access.
