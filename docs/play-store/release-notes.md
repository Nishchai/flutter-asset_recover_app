# Release notes — Asset Recovery 1.0.0 (build 101)

Play Console "What's new" limit: 500 characters per language.
Have a native speaker review each translation before publishing.

## English (en-US)
```
First release of Asset Recovery for Sun Mobility field agents.
• Work your assigned recovery tickets and navigate to the customer
• Verify your location with GPS geo-fence checks
• Call customers from the app; call duration is logged
• Scan asset QR codes and capture geo-tagged proof photos
• Works offline: updates sync automatically when you're back online
• Available in English, Hindi, Kannada, Marathi, Tamil and Telugu
```
